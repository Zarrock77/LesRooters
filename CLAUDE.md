## Task 3 — Unlearning

Files: `huggingface.co/datasets/SprintML/unlearning` (`model.pt`, `forget_images.npz`, `task_template.py`, `submission.py`).
- Model: timm `vit_base_patch16_224`, 100 classes, LayerScale init 1e-4. Use the builder and preprocessing from `task_template.py` (the server uses the same).
- Forget set: `forget_images.npz["images"]`, uint8 `[3500,224,224,3]`, raw and unnormalized, **no labels**. No other training data is provided.
- Target behaviour = a model retrained without the forget set (the "gold" models), measured on the forget images and on hidden altered copies of them. The goal is not to maximize loss on the forget set.
- Score = forget × retain:
  - forget = mean_std_score (the forget-set loss mean and std versus gold) × prediction_score (per-image KL(gold‖sub) at softmax T=2). Both are normalized by the original model's distance to gold.
  - retain = clip(acc_sub / acc_orig) × 1/(1 + mean KL(orig‖sub)) on a hidden control set. Any drift from the original model on non-forget data costs points.
  - Gate: <70% accuracy on the hidden validation set gives a score of 0.
- Submission: a float32 state dict that loads with `strict=True` into the original architecture (merge any LoRA or adapters back in), no NaN/Inf, ≤450 MB. The server evaluates in fp16 autocast.
- **T3 status (2026-11-21 ~21h):** work lives in `Unlearning/` (`unlearn.py`, Slurm configs in `jureca/`); on JURECA in `/p/scratch/training2666/gras1/LesRooters/unlearning/jj/`. `Unlearning/README.md` has the full score table and leads; update it after every submission. Best public score 0.0597 (`sub9_0`): fine-tune the last 6 blocks toward a "smoothed teacher" (orig softmax averaged over 16 aggressive crops, scale 0.05–0.35) sharpened ×20 in log space, plus a KL(orig‖model) anchor on mixup images. Sharpening helped monotonically (×3 0.031, ×10 0.055, ×20 0.060), with diminishing returns. JiT (Foster et al. 2024) as reimplemented collapsed the model. Submission cooldown ≈ 10 min.
